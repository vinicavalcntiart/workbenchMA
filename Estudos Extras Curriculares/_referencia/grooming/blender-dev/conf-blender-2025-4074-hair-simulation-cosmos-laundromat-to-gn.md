Hair Simulation: From Cosmos Laundromat to Geometry Nodes
Development of hair simulation in Blender since Cosmos Laundromat and its integration with geometry nodes. Why we are working on a new hair simulation system, what we've learned from past experience, and how we want to make it work for artists.
