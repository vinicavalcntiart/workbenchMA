Hi Frank,

Kool Produktion released Ella Bella Bingo as a feature in 2020 and Ella Bella Bingo Racer as a game in the same year. Same cast, two very different destinations. That crossing is the exact shape of my own career, which is why I am writing ☺️

I am Vini Cavalcanti, a Senior 3D Character Artist with more than ten years in stylized characters, MA candidate in Creative Industries and Game Art Specialist (PG Dip). On The Wingfeather Saga season 1 at Angel Studios I modeled and hand-painted the show's characters for screen. For almost five years at E-Line Media I have carried Endstar's hero characters from first sculpt into engine.

A character that has to work in a film and in a game is not two jobs, but it is two budgets. The film wants the sculpt to carry the close-up, the game wants topology and texture that hold at a fraction of the cost, and the face has to stay the same face in both. Building for that constraint from the start is cheaper than retrofitting it later.

With The Legend of Magnus the Good in development, characters are what gets built first.

If character or modeling work opens up on your side, I'd like to be on your list. And if someone else there is the right person for this, just point me and I will take it there.
