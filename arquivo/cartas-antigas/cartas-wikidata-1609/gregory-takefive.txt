Hi Gregory,

Carbone 14 is animating Nino Dino in Blender, 78 episodes of seven minutes. A cast that has to survive that many episodes is a build problem before it is a drawing problem, and that is what I do ☺️

I am Vini Cavalcanti, a Senior 3D Character Artist with more than ten years in stylized characters, MA candidate in Creative Industries and Game Art Specialist (PG Dip). On The Wingfeather Saga season 1 at Angel Studios I modeled and hand-painted the show's characters for screen. For almost five years at E-Line Media I have carried Endstar's hero characters from first sculpt into engine, through retopology, UVs, baking, texturing, look development and grooming in Houdini.

Long-form series punish shortcuts taken at the start. Topology that deforms cleanly, UVs that survive a redesign, and a groom that renders at episode eighty the way it did at episode one are cheaper to build once than to repair across a season.

I know Take Five produces and Carbone 14 animates, and that I am writing to the production side. That is deliberate: the people who plan a series are the ones who decide how early the cast has to be solved.

If character or modeling work opens up on your side, I'd like to be on your list. And if someone else there is the right person for this, just point me and I will take it there.
