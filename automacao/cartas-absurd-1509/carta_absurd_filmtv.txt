Said first, because it decides the rest: I am not authorized to work in the United States and I would need visa sponsorship. I am ready to move to Santa Monica or San Rafael for the role.

I am writing to the Film and TV side because of what changes for a character artist when the destination is a screen rather than a controller. In a game the asset survives a thousand angles the player chooses. In film it faces one camera, chosen on purpose, held longer and seen larger. The budget moves from breadth to depth, and the sculpt stops being a base for texture and becomes the performance.

That is the work I want to own. I am a Senior 3D Character Artist with more than ten years in stylized characters, MA candidate in Creative Industries and Game Art Specialist (PG Dip). I take a character the whole way: sculpt and high poly, retopology that holds under deformation, UVs, baking, hand-painted and PBR texturing, look development, cloth in Marvelous Designer and grooming in Houdini.

I have shipped in both destinations, which is why I write here and not only to the game side. On The Wingfeather Saga season 1 at Angel Studios I modeled and hand-painted the show's characters for screen. For almost five years at E-Line Media I have carried Endstar's hero characters from first sculpt into engine.
